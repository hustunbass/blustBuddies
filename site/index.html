Boom PalsBomber Party

Drop bombs, chain explosions and outsmart the robots. A fast, colourful arcade maze game for you and up to 6 friends online.

Coming soon to the App Store

Features

  • 100 story levels in 10 worlds, 3-star ratings and a boss every 25 levels
  • Battle bots (easy, normal, hard), 2 vs 2 teams and a daily arena
  • Online battles for up to 7 players: Quick match, room codes, password rooms and emotes
  • Rejoin a running online match if your connection drops
  • Wardrobe with hats, faces and colours, daily quests and achievements
  • 7 power-ups: extra bombs, longer blasts, speed, shield, bomb kick, detonator and ghost
  • English and Turkish

Free to play, with ads and optional in-app purchases. Story and bot battles work offline.
