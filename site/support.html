Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the shop (cart button on the main menu) and tap Restore purchases. This brings back "Remove ads". Coins are consumable and cannot be restored by the store.

Where is my progress stored?

Progress, coins and cosmetics are stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes them unless your device backup restores the app data. Use the same device backup when switching phones.

Online mode does not connect or takes a long time.

The game server may need up to a minute to wake up if nobody played for a while; the app shows "Waking up the server". If it still fails, check your internet connection and try again.

My connection dropped during an online match.

Stay in the match screen: the game tries to reconnect for about 25 seconds and your pal keeps its seat for 30 seconds.

I want to remove ads.

Buy Remove ads in the shop. Optional rewarded ads (for coins and bonuses) stay available.

I was charged but did not receive my coins.

Restart the app and open the shop once; the store delivers pending purchases. If coins are still missing, email us the order number from Apple's receipt.
