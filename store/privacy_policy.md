## Summary
Blast Buddies does not collect, sell or share personal data. The only data
that leaves your device is described under "Online play" and is used solely to
run the match.

## Data stored on your device
The game stores only your settings (sound, music, vibration, control size) and
progress (unlocked levels, best score) locally using the platform's
preferences storage. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you
uninstall the app. The display name and server address you enter for online
play are also stored locally so you don't have to retype them.

## Online play
Online play is optional. When you use it, the app connects to the game server
whose address you enter and sends the display name you choose, room names and
passwords you type, and your in-game inputs (movement and bombs). The server
keeps this data in memory only while you are connected and does not store it
afterwards; room passwords are kept only as salted hashes. Other players in
the same room see your display name. On iOS the app may ask for local network
access so it can reach a server on your Wi-Fi.

## Data we do not collect
No account, email, location, contacts, advertising identifier, analytics or
crash reports. The app contains no ads or third-party tracking SDKs.
